🎓 AI-Powered Student College Help Desk

A Centralized Platform for College Information, Student Services, and Academic Support

📌 Project Overview

The AI-Powered Student College Help Desk is a platform designed to simplify college life by bringing essential college information, student services, and academic support into one place.

Visitors can explore general college information without logging in, while registered students can access additional services such as AI-powered assistance, library information, faculty details, campus locations, holiday schedules, fee deposit status, and assignment submission.

The platform also supports teachers in reviewing student assignments and providing feedback.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Centralize College Information: Provide a single platform for accessing essential college information and services.
- Enable AI-Powered Assistance: Help students find answers to common college-related questions.
- Improve Accessibility: Allow visitors to access general college information without registration.
- Simplify Student Services: Make academic and administrative information easier to find.
- Support Academic Workflows: Facilitate assignment submission, teacher reviews, and feedback.
- Improve Campus Navigation: Help students locate academic blocks, hostels, and other campus facilities.

✨ Key Features

1. Public College Information

Visitors can access essential information without creating an account.

- College introduction and general information
- College vision and objectives
- Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)
- Registration and login options

2. Student Registration and Login

Students can create an account and log in to access student-specific services.

- Student registration
- Login to the student portal
- Access to available student services

3. AI-Powered Student Help Desk

The platform provides AI-powered assistance for college-related queries.

Example questions include:

- Where is the academic block located?
- Is a particular book available in the library?
- Where can I find faculty information?
- How can I check my fee deposit status?

4. Library Information and Book Availability

Students can access library-related information and check book availability before visiting the library.

5. Faculty Information

Provides access to faculty-related details, helping students find relevant academic information more conveniently.

6. College Holiday Information

Students can check college holiday schedules to plan their academic activities and campus visits.

7. Campus Infrastructure and Location Assistance

Helps students locate important campus facilities, including:

- Academic blocks
- Hostels
- Other college infrastructure

8. Student Query Submission

Students can raise queries related to college information and services when they need further assistance.

9. Assignment Submission

Students can submit academic assignments through the platform for teacher review.

10. Teacher Assignment Review and Feedback

Teachers can review submitted assignments and provide feedback to students.

Workflow:

Student submits assignment → Teacher reviews submission → Teacher provides feedback → Student receives feedback.

11. Fee Deposit Status

Students can check their fee deposit status through the portal without making a separate inquiry at the accounts office.

Note: This feature concerns fee status information and does not imply that the platform processes payments.

🔄 How the Platform Works

Visitor Workflow

1. Open the college help desk.
2. View general college information.
3. Explore the college vision and FAQs.
4. Register or log in to access additional services.

Student Workflow

1. Register for an account.
2. Log in to the student portal.
3. Access the AI-powered help desk.
4. Explore library, faculty, holiday, and campus information.
5. Raise queries or submit assignments.
6. Check fee deposit status.

Teacher Workflow

1. Access the teacher-facing assignment workflow.
2. Review student submissions.
3. Provide feedback on assignments.
4. Share feedback with students through the platform.

🔒 Security and Data Privacy

Because the platform may handle student accounts, assignments, and fee-related information, important security considerations include:

- Keeping API keys and credentials out of public repositories.
- Protecting student records and personal information.
- Restricting access to authorized teacher functions.
- Validating submitted data and assignment files.
- Using anonymized or test data in public demonstrations.

These are recommended security practices and do not necessarily indicate that every control is already implemented.
